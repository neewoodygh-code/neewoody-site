Built in genuine West African hardwood.

We work in timber that grows here, chosen for their durability, grain character, and how they age in the Accra climate. The wood is part of the brief.

Afromosia

Pericopsis elata

A premium West African hardwood with natural oil content, fine grain, and a warm honey tone. Highly durable, stable in humid climates, and an exceptional finish timber. Often specified for statement dining tables, live-edge pieces, and outdoor-facing installations.

Hyedua

Guibourtia ehie

A dense, heavy hardwood with a naturally deep, warm tone and tight even grain. Takes a dark stain evenly and richly, which suits dining sets, console pieces, and full room commissions where a unified, premium finish across multiple pieces is the brief.
