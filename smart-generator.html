Smart Generator (experimental)

Describe a wardrobe or a kitchen. Every part is placed in 3D first and the cut list is read off the model, so the two always agree and parts that run into each other are flagged.

EXPERIMENTAL: check every size before cutting. Made with the Carpentry Concierge Smart Generator, which is under construction. Neewoody Custom Woodwork and Carpentry Concierge accept no responsibility for material cut or work done from these figures.

Kitchen

Base units stand on legs. The worktop's doubled 4 cm edge sits flush with the carcass top, so base sides are the worktop height less 40 less the legs.

Runs

A run is one line of units along a wall, base or wall cabinets. Units go left to right as you face the wall. On a side wall, "starts at" is measured from the back wall. Drawer heights are shared out to fit the unit. A hob cut-out depth of 0 means no hob; a return depth of 0 means the same depth as the run. A gap leaves space for an appliance.

Construction (your own settings)

Remembered on this device. The J-profile height is used only when the handles are J-profile. Backs are screwed on; the sink unit has none.

Size

Sections are shared out across the carcasses from left to right. Inside one carcass, sections are split by dividers.

Sections, left to right

Spaces are listed top to bottom. The top space takes whatever height is left.

Construction (your own settings)

Remembered on this device. Sizes in mm. Set back 0 = toe kick flush with the front.

Cut list

Edge banding comes on as standard (doors and fronts all round, the front edge of carcass parts and shelves). In the Cut Sheet, tap an edge to take off what this job doesn't need. The Cut Sheet also works out the boards. Client 3D and SketchUp files are for members.
