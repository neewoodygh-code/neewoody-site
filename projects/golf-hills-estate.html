A complete kitchen dining area

The client wanted to turn a bare kitchen dining area in their Golf Hills Estate home into one working space. The brief covered everything the room needed: a dining table and bench for the open side, a built-in L-shaped banquette to wrap the corner, and a timber-framed mirror above the banquette to open up the space.

All four pieces were designed to read as one installation. The stained pine tops and matte black X-frame legs give the set a clean, industrial edge, while the black leatherette on the banquette ties the seating together and hides the storage the client needed.

Everything was built in the Neewoody workshop, delivered, and installed in a single site visit.

The full scope

Four pieces, built and installed as one dining area.

X-frame dining table

Stained pine top on solid matte black X-frame legs, built to the room's dimensions and made to pair with the bench and banquette.

Matching bench

Stained pine bench on matching X-frame legs, sized to run the full length of the open side of the table.

Built-in L-shaped banquette

Corner banquette in black leatherette with storage under the seats, built into the corner of the dining area.

Timber-framed wall mirror

A solid timber frame mirror above the banquette, which adds depth to the corner and finishes the fitted look.

The kitchen cabinets and worktops in the background of some photos were already there and were not part of this commission. Our scope was the dining table, bench, banquette and mirror only.
