One house, one look, room by room.

This was a new house in Bortianor, and the brief covered the fitted joinery from the kitchen to the master bathroom. Warm walnut-grain fronts run through every room, set against charcoal, cream and dark veined worktops, so the house reads as one piece.

Lighting was planned into the units from the start: strips in the TV-wall shelving, along the wardrobe frames and under the bar shelves, so the woodwork is lit at night as well as by day.

The kitchen

Tall walnut-grain units and wall cabinets with charcoal base units below, an island with deep drawers, and dark veined worktops along the run.

Living room and bar

A cream TV wall with fluted panels, framed by lit walnut-grain shelving, with a low run of cabinets beneath. Across the room, a gentleman’s bar in dark wood with open shelves above the counter.

Master bedroom

A second TV wall in stone-look panels with walnut-grain strips and a matching low cabinet, opposite a full-height wardrobe with black-framed glass doors and lit shelves inside.

Pantry

Tall dark woodgrain cupboards on two walls, with open shelves in the corner for everyday things.

Master bathroom

A floating walnut-grain vanity with a dark veined top, matching the kitchen, under a frameless mirror.
