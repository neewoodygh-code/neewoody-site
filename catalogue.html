The Workshop

Made to measure, not made to fit.

We are a furniture and carpentry workshop on Amugi Avenue in Accra, trading since 2015. Every job starts with the room: its size, its light and how it will be used. We draw it, cut it, finish it and fit it with our own crew.

We work in laminate and in solid Ghanaian hardwood: fitted wardrobes and kitchens, beds, TV walls, dining rooms, live-edge tables, and whole houses fitted out at once. The photos are of real work in clients' homes and premises. The one visualisation, on page 11, is marked as one.

4.8 out of 5 from 14 Google reviews. We have built and installed from East Legon to the Volta Region.

  1. Measure

    We come to the room and take every dimension ourselves.

  2. Draw and price

    A drawing, the finishes and one clear price, valid 14 days.

  3. Build

    Cut, assembled and finished in our Accra workshop.

  4. Fit

    Our own crew installs it and leaves the room clean.
