The Workshop

Furniture made to measure, not made to fit.

Neewoody Custom Woodwork is a bespoke furniture and carpentry workshop based on Amugi Avenue in Accra. Every commission begins with the room it is built for: its dimensions, its light, the way it will be used. It is then drawn, cut, finished and installed by our own crew.

We work in laminate and solid hardwood alike: fitted wardrobes and kitchens, dining sets and beds, live-edge tables, and full institutional fitouts. The pages that follow show real installations in clients' homes and premises, with no renders and no stock photography.

Clients rate us 4.8 out of 5 on Google, from 14 reviews. We have designed, built and installed by hand from East Legon to the Volta Region.
