Common questions about custom beds.

How long does it take to build a custom bed?
Most bed frames take 2 to 4 weeks from order confirmation to delivery. More complex builds with storage, upholstery, or intricate detailing may take up to 6 weeks. We'll give you a realistic timeline when you confirm your order.
Can you match an existing bedroom set?
Yes. If you have existing bedroom furniture, share photos and dimensions with us. We'll match the stain colour, wood grain, and finish as closely as possible so the new piece blends in seamlessly.
Do you build beds for children?
Absolutely. We build bunk beds, cabin beds, and loft beds with children's safety as a top priority: solid joints, fitted guard rails, and smooth edges throughout.
What mattress sizes do you build for?
We build for any mattress size: standard single (90×190cm), double (135×190cm), queen (150×200cm), king (160×200cm), or completely custom dimensions. Tell us your mattress size and we build around it.
Can I add a headboard later?
It's easier and often cheaper to include the headboard in the original build. Retrofitting requires re-working the frame. We'd recommend deciding upfront. Show us inspiration photos and we'll quote both options.
Do you deliver and assemble in Accra?
Yes. Delivery and assembly is included for all locations within Accra. We bring the pieces, assemble the bed in your room, and remove any packaging. For areas outside Accra, contact us for a delivery quote.
